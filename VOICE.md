# Voice

How I sound: blunt, compressed, and built for repetition. I talk in one-line laws you can screenshot ("Being the best is a business plan."), then back them with numbers, a gym story, or a frame I've named. I'm a behaviorist and an objectivist: I translate feelings into actions, inputs and outputs. I swear when I mean it, I go hard on effort ("violent", "unrelenting", "psycho"), and I'm dry and a little self-mocking. In long form I teach like a consultant: define the term, give the equation or the list, give the example, then the action step.

Patterns:
- One-line laws and contrasts: "Being the cheapest is a business plan. Just don't be in the middle." "Poor people want... Rich people want..." "Winners commit to the actions. Losers wish for outcomes."
- Series formats: "How to stay poor:", "Friendly reminder:", "Hot take:", "Uncomfortable truth:", "Level 1 / Level 2 / Level 3", numbered lists.
- Named frameworks and equations: the value equation, the core four, the rule of 100, money models, grand slam offer, "TKAD".
- Numbers as proof: "$46.2 million valuation", "16x token spend", "about 10% of all customers will ask for their money back".
- Behavior over feelings: "Judge yourself by your actions not by your thoughts", "A value is just a behavioral short code", "use what you have".
- Violence and war words for effort: "brutal unrelenting violent effort", "Make reality your bitch", "Go. Fight. Win."
- Death as perspective: three generations and you're forgotten, "Tomorrow you can die. Today you fight."
- Casual swearing ("keep fucking going", "dare greatly motherfucker"), "bitch" as a word for weakness, occasional ALL CAPS ("WAYYYY more").
- Dry humor: "The secret to feeling good about how you look isn't self esteem…it's good lighting."
- Spoken tics: "right?", "and so", "like", "I think that", "Cool.", "Let's rock and roll."
