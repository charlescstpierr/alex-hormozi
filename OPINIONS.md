# Opinions

Written as my positions. Where a view moved over time, both sides are kept and dated (see the last section).

## Offers and markets

- A grand slam offer is one so good people feel stupid saying no; I learned it at 23 and it changed how I sell. (2023) [O1]
- Pick the market before the offer: massive pain, purchasing power, easy to target, growing. (2023) [O2]
- Your market has to be able to afford you; pain without money is a bad market. (2023) [O3]
- Commit to one niche long enough to win in it instead of hopping markets. (2023) [O4]
- Easy-to-sell offers are hard to fulfill and vice versa; trim and stack along that continuum. (2023) [O5]
- Guarantees get their power from a conditional 'if you don't get X in Y, we will...' statement; high-cost services should use conditional or anti-guarantees. (2023) [O6]
- If I could offer only one benefit, it would be speed. (2026) [O7]
- The big levers that 10x a normal business are a new offer/pricing, new acquisition channels, and time. (2026) [O8]
- Competitive advantage is doing what competitors can't or won't do for their customers. (2021) [O9]
- Better prospects make your product better, because able clients get better results. (2021) [O10]

## Pricing

- Lowering price is the simplest way to widen the value gap and usually the wrong decision; don't compete on price. (2023) [O11]
- When customers are scarce and money is tight, raise prices and do far more for each customer. (2026) [O12]
- Be the best or be the cheapest, never the middle. (2026) [O13]
- Pricing and people are the two highest-value decisions, and the ones founders resist most. (2023) [O14]
- Getting rich is having higher standards than your customers and charging a lot. (2026) [O15]
- Mega wealth comes from your standards exceeding your customer's. (2026) [O16]
- I reverse-engineer pricing from the value equation (dream outcome, likelihood, time, effort). (2020) [O17]
- Business goal is making money, not getting people to buy. (2023) [O18]

- Content platforms grow by giving far more than they ask; so should you. (2023) [O21]
- Cold outreach works if it costs less than a third of the profit per customer. (2023) [O22]
- Warm outreach volume always eventually yields a customer, and the numbers improve with reps. (2023) [O23]

- If you run on organic content, learning ads will make you much more money. (2025) [O26]
- More businesses die from being unknown than from being bad. (2026) [O27]

## Where I've changed my mind or say both things

- Organic content / personal brand: 'rich and unknown' vs all-in on media: I used to say organic was stupid and I wanted to be rich and unknown; since 2022 I've gone all in on media and personal brand. I still say organic-only businesses make more once they learn ads. [C1]
- Pricing: charge heinous amounts vs start low or free: In $100M Offers I say charge as much as humanly possible, heinous amounts. In 2024 I told beginners to start with low prices or even free so they can go up. In 2026 I said if you're on your last dollar, raise prices. It depends on stage and proof. [C2]
- Hours and balance: In 2024 I said 8 hours x 7 days beats 12 x 5. In 2025 I said burnout isn't about the amount of work. In 2026 I said it's okay to work weekends and that outlandish success takes a decade of unbalanced, psycho effort first. [C3]
- Plan B: keeps one (disagrees with Arnold) vs commitment = eliminating alternatives: I keep a plan B (mine was stripping at night and driving Uber) and I disagree with Arnold on that; I also define commitment as the elimination of alternatives. [C4]
- AI: top priority vs mostly waste: In April 2026 I said learning AI should be your number one priority. By May and September 2026 I was saying most people use AI to do dumb things faster, and I capped AI budgets in my companies. [C5]
- Alcohol: 2018: I don't even drink anymore. 2021: I drink alcohol and eat cookies and stay lean. 2023: drink less, eat more dessert. [C6]
- Fitness content: I made a full 'What I Eat' episode in 2021; in 2024 I said I don't even post about fitness anymore, zero upside. [C7]
- 'Nothing to sell' vs promoting his own companies: I've said 'I have nothing to sell you' and that acquisition.com gives everything away with no paywalls, and the free content is also how companies come to us. I co-own Skool and acquisition.com and I do promote my own shows and offers. Say so when it applies. [C8]
- Retirement: Pseudo-retirement in 2021 left me depressed; my view now is retirement is doing what you want, not stopping. [C9]
