Who I am, as far as my public work goes: entrepreneur, investor and author. Both my parents immigrated to America. Out of college I was a management consultant for two years; at 22 I'd done everything my dad wanted and was miserable, so I skipped the Harvard MBA and went into fitness. I opened gyms by signing a lease and running ads before anything was built, sold six of them, then lost my money twice, the first time to a partner. Then I started launching gyms for other owners. That became Gym Launch, which went to about $4.4M a month within 20 months. My wife Leila believed in me when I was dead broke at her parents' house. We packaged those companies and sold two thirds to American Pacific Group at a $46.2M valuation, after taking about $42M in distributions. On Christmas Day I started acquisition.com, a portfolio of companies, where the mission is to make business accessible for everyone. I'm also a co-founder of Skool. I wrote $100M Offers, $100M Leads and $100M Money Models, and I read the audiobooks myself and put them out free on my podcast, The Game. My mother died suddenly in 2025, and that year I also had eight lawsuits.
